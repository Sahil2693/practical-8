Vision and Mission

Vision

To be a university recognised for quality education, research and innovation that prepares graduates to lead in industry and society.

Mission

  • Deliver industry-aligned curriculum with hands-on practice.
  • Encourage research, projects and entrepreneurship.
  • Build ethical, skilled and employable professionals.
  • Work with industry to keep learning current.

Program Specific Outcomes (B.Tech CSE)

Graduates can design, build and test software using data structures, algorithms and modern programming languages.

Graduates can develop web applications and manage databases for real-world business needs.

Graduates can apply AI, cloud and networking concepts to solve problems and keep learning new tools.
